Navigating Superannuation, GST, and PSI as an Australian Contractor

Contractors transitioning from permanent PAYG employment must properly adjust their financial model to compensate for statutory employment protections:

  • Self-Funded Superannuation (11.5%–12%): Unlike employees whose employers legally contribute to their super fund, sole traders must budget and contribute voluntarily. Building a 12% super allocation into your rate prevents a severe shortfall in retirement savings while qualifying for tax deductions up to annual concessional caps.
  • The A$75,000 GST Threshold: When your rolling 12-month turnover reaches $75,000 AUD, you must register for GST with the ATO. You then collect 10% GST on taxable invoices and remit it via your quarterly Business Activity Statement (BAS). You can offset this against GST paid on business software, hardware, and mobile expenses.
  • Personal Services Income (PSI) Rules: Setting up a Pty Ltd company does not automatically allow you to pay the 25% base rate corporate tax if your income is earned primarily through your personal skills and efforts. If your contracts fail the ATO's PSI tests, the net income is attributed directly to you at individual marginal rates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How should Australian sole traders factor Superannuation into their freelance rates?

While sole traders are not legally required by the ATO to pay Superannuation Guarantee to themselves, failing to do so sacrifices retirement wealth. Full-time employees receive 11.5% to 12.0% Super on top of their base salary. Australian freelancers should factor an additional 11.5%–12% voluntary contribution directly into their baseline day or hourly rate.

When does an Australian freelancer need to register for GST?

Under ATO rules, you must register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) within 21 days if your gross business turnover reaches or is expected to reach $75,000 AUD in a 12-month period. Once registered, you must add 10% GST to your invoices and lodge quarterly Business Activity Statements (BAS).

What is the Personal Services Income (PSI) test for Australian contractors?

The ATO's PSI rules prevent individuals from using a company (Pty Ltd) structure or trust solely to split income or pay lower corporate tax rates on income derived mainly from personal labor. To be classified as a Personal Services Business (PSB), you must pass the Results Test, or the 80% rule combined with unrelated clients, employment, or business premises tests.

What is the average freelance contractor day rate in Australia in 2026?

In 2026, Australian IT and software contractors bill between A$750 and A$1,350 per day (A$95–A$170/hr). Creative, digital marketing, and design contractors bill A$550 to A$950 per day (A$70–A$120/hr), while management and strategic advisors bill A$1,200 to A$2,200+ per day.

Can Australian sole traders deduct home office running expenses on their tax return?

Yes. The ATO allows sole traders to claim deductions for working from home using either the fixed rate method (67 cents per hour covering electricity, internet, mobile phone, and stationery) or the actual cost method based on dedicated workspace records and receipts.
