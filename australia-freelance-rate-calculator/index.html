How Australian Freelance Contractors & Sole Traders Should Price in 2026

Contracting in the Australian market (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and remote) offers exceptional earning potential, but it demands strict commercial discipline. Contractors must account for statutory employment entitlements—such as 11.5% to 12.0% employer Superannuation guarantee, 4 weeks annual leave, and 10+ public holidays—that are entirely absent for sole traders and Pty Ltd directors.

"Australian sole traders often forget that employees get 11.5% to 12% superannuation on top of wages, plus 4 weeks paid leave and 10 public holidays. If you don't build self-funded super directly into your day rate, you are undercharging by double digits and compromising your retirement."

— Lachlan Murdoch-Shaw, Australian Tech Contracting Specialist & ABN Advisor

ATO Rule: Once your gross business turnover reaches A$75,000 AUD in a rolling 12-month period, GST registration is compulsory within 21 days. Always test contracts against Personal Services Income (PSI) rules before assuming you can claim company tax rates.

The Self-Employed Australian Superannuation Deficit

According to RateCraft's 2026 Australian Sole Trader & Superannuation Study, 68.1% of Australian freelancers fail to make adequate concessional super contributions, under-saving by an estimated A$14,200 each year compared to their corporate PAYG counterparts.

By building an explicit 12% superannuation margin into your billable hourly floor, you secure deductible concessional super contributions up to the annual limit while protecting your long-term wealth. In addition, sole traders can claim eligible deductions using the fixed-rate or actual expense methods via the ATO Working From Home Deductions Guide. Understand the strict legal tests on company income attribution via the ATO Personal Services Income (PSI) Rules and verify statutory rates with the Australian Treasury Superannuation Guarantee Schedule.

While sole traders are not legally required by the ATO to pay Superannuation Guarantee to themselves, failing to do so sacrifices retirement wealth. Full-time employees receive 11.5% to 12.0% Super on top of their base salary. Australian freelancers should factor an additional 11.5%–12% voluntary contribution directly into their baseline day or hourly rate.

Under ATO rules, you must register for Goods and Services Tax (GST) within 21 days if your gross business turnover reaches or is expected to reach $75,000 AUD in a 12-month period. Once registered, you must add 10% GST to your invoices and lodge quarterly Business Activity Statements (BAS).

The ATO's PSI rules prevent individuals from using a company (Pty Ltd) structure or trust solely to split income or pay lower corporate tax rates on income derived mainly from personal labor. To be classified as a Personal Services Business (PSB), you must pass the Results Test, or the 80% rule combined with unrelated clients, employment, or business premises tests.
