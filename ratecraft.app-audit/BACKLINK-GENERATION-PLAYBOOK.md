## 💬 4. Community Q&A Snippet (Reddit / Quora / Forums)

Use when replying to threads asking *"How much should I charge as a freelancer?"* in `r/freelance`, `r/webdev`, or `r/Upwork`:

```text
Remember that only 50%–60% of your weekly hours are actually billable. If your target income is $80k, dividing by 2,080 hours ($38/hr) will lose you money because self-employment taxes take 15%–30% and proposals/invoicing take 15+ hours weekly.

Formula:
Rate = (Target Income / (1 - Tax Rate) + Overhead + 20% Profit) / (Billable Weeks * Billable Hours/Week)

For an $80k take-home, a sustainable rate is typically $75–$95/hr. 
You can run your exact numbers for free on RateCraft (https://ratecraft.app) — it's browser-based with zero signup.
```
