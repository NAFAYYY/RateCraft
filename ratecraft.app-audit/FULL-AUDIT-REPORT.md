## 3. Technical SEO (Score: 99/100)

### What Works
- **Self-Referential Canonicals:** 100% (45/45) of pages feature self-referential canonical tags with canonical HTTPS URLs and standardized trailing slashes.
- **Hreflang Reciprocity & Symmetry:** 100% reciprocal hreflang pairings between all English (`en`) and Spanish (`es`) URLs, ensuring flawless search engine indexation in US, UK, EU, and Latin American SERPs.
- **Robots.txt Architecture:** Universal crawl permissions (`Allow: /`), explicit permissions for AI engines (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended`), and correct embed protection (`Disallow: /*?*embed=*`). Direct reference to `sitemap.xml`.
- **Sitemap Health:** Complete, XML-compliant `sitemap.xml` listing 45 live URLs with priorities and bidirectional hreflang links.
- **Security & Edge Headers:** Cloudflare edge enforces HTTPS, HSTS, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, and `Content-Signal: ai-train=yes, search=yes`.

### Optimization Notes
- Add `rel="nofollow"` to the footer sitemap link (`<a href="...sitemap.xml" rel="nofollow">`) to prevent search crawlers from treating the machine feed as a prospective HTML search result.
