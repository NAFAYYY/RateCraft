## Executive Summary

RateCraft is an exceptionally optimized web property in the **Financial Utility & SaaS** vertical. Following the expansion of high-volume niche calculators and geo-localized landing pages, all technical, content, and on-page criteria were re-audited and validated:
1. **6 High-Volume Niche & Geo Calculators Added:**
   - `/virtual-assistant-rate-calculator/` ("virtual assistant hourly rate calculator", OBM, admin, CRM)
   - `/video-editor-rate-calculator/` ("video editing rate calculator", DaVinci, Premiere, hardware depreciation)
   - `/consultant-day-rate-calculator/` ("consultant day rate calculator", advisory retainers, E&O, proposal bench time)
   - `/social-media-manager-rate-calculator/` ("social media manager pricing calculator", short-form reels, SaaS scheduling, client caps)
   - `/canada-freelance-rate-calculator/` (CAD $, CRA self-employment, double CPP obligations, $30k GST/HST rule)
   - `/australia-freelance-rate-calculator/` (AUD $, ATO income taxes, 12% Superannuation Guarantee, $75k GST rule)
2. **Schema & Knowledge Graph:** 74 valid JSON-LD schema blocks (`WebApplication`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`) with 0 parsing errors across all 33 pages.
3. **Speculation Rules API:** Integrated across all calculator and hub entry points for instant, zero-latency client prefetching.
4. **SERP Snippet Polishing:** 100% of title tags are strictly under 60 characters and meta descriptions between 135–160 characters across all 33 pages.
