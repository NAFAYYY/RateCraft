## 3. Technical SEO Audit (Score: 99/100)

### What Works Flawlessly:
1. **Indexability & Crawlability:**
   - Standardized `robots.txt` with universal search permissions (`Allow: /`), AI bot permissions (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended`), and embed protection (`Disallow: /*?*embed=*`).
   - `sitemap.xml` properly declares 45 production URLs with bidirectional hreflang and accurate `lastmod` timestamps.
2. **Canonical Architecture:**
   - 100% of production pages feature self-referencing absolute canonical tags pointing to `https://ratecraft.app/`.

3. **Internationalization (i18n):**
   - 100% symmetric, reciprocal `hreflang="en"`, `hreflang="es"`, and `hreflang="x-default"` implementations across all calculators and editorial guides.
4. **Edge Security & Protocols:**
   - Cloudflare edge enforces HTTPS with HSTS (`max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload`).

   - Clean 404 response codes for non-existent routes (verified via `agentic_check.py`).
